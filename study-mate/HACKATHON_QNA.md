# PrepPulse AI — Hackathon Q&A Guide

## What is PrepPulse AI?
PrepPulse AI is an agentic Study GPS for JEE/NEET aspirants. It tells students what to study today, what to revise, which concepts are weak, which mistakes repeat, and how exam-ready they are.

## What problem does it solve?
Most students work hard but study randomly. PrepPulse AI converts preparation into a personalized, data-driven workflow using profile data, uploaded resources, quiz performance, mistake memory, revision alerts, and readiness analytics.

## Why is it different?
It is not only a notes app or chatbot. It combines:
- Student onboarding
- RAG from uploaded notes
- AI Doubt Solver
- YouTube Learning Companion
- Adaptive Quiz Generator
- Mistake Memory
- Today’s Battle Plan
- Exam Readiness Score
- Weak Concept Heatmap

## Is it demo-ready without a local AI model?
Yes. The backend first tries an Ollama-compatible local model. If the model is unavailable, clean fallback responses keep the full demo flow working.

## What is the safety disclaimer?
AI-generated explanations should be verified for high-stakes exam preparation.
